# DL-2026-0001 — Shift from Agent Identification to Fragment Reconstruction

## Decision

The case will prioritize reconstruction of fragmented operational patterns over identification of individual agents.

## Rationale

Individual identification is often the most visible part of reporting, but it can obscure the structural question: whether apparently independent low-level tasks share documented infrastructure, intermediaries, timing or objectives.

## Constraint

No relationship will be inferred solely from nationality, platform, language, similar task, media description or alleged affiliation.

## Success condition

A useful result can be a negative finding:

> **Multiple fragments were investigated, but public evidence was insufficient to establish that they belonged to the same operational cycle.**

That result is analytically valid and protects against over-attribution.

## Overview (indictment language)

Since at least in or about 2024, a network of individuals working for the intelligence services of the Russian Federation (the **"RIS Network"**) has conspired to conduct and conducted attacks and related activity.

Three high-ranking members identified: YURI KHRAMEEV (former colonel, Russian intelligence services); KIRILL KHRAMEEV (FSB officer, son of Yuri); OEMIS ROMAGOZA DURRUTHY (Cuban national, influential member of Cuban diaspora in Russia, coordinated attacks for the RIS Network).

YAIDEL DELGADO SUAREZ a/k/a "Viking" described as prolific recruiter; with CASTRO and YURI KHRAMEEV recruited individual living in the United States for assassination of prominent Russian dissident.

## Durruthy / Associate-1 — Prague (June 2024)

**Indictment text (public excerpt):**

> In or about June 2024, DURRUTHY paid and coordinated travel for a criminal associate ("**Associate-1**") to carry out attacks in Prague, Czechia.

**Consistent secondary detail (OCCRP / Czech reporting, not in indictment body as name):**
- Associate-1 = **Andrés Alfonso de la Hoz de la Cruz** (Colombian; convicted Prague Municipal Court June 2025, 8 years).
- Durruthy transferred **over $1,000 in cryptocurrency** and **booked hotels in Poland and Czechia** for Associate-1 ahead of the operation.

**Cross-link to Case 004:** Czech court independently recorded instructions via Telegram from person using nickname **Adrian** — matches Romagoza alias set.

## Durruthy — Lithuania (September 2024)

Public reporting on indictment: Durruthy’s logistical support continued through the fall, **coordinating a separate attack in Lithuania in September 2024** (aligns with Šiauliai / TVC Solutions timeline 17–22 Sep 2024).

## US-focused plot (Suarez / Castro / Khrameev)

- Recruit of **U.S. Resident-1** for pre-operational surveillance of **Victim-1** (Russian dissident, Washington area).
- Offers reported: $1,000–$1,500 for surveillance; **$40,000** to "eliminate" or "disappear" Victim-1.
- Separate narrative: offer ~$25,000 related to a person living in **Lithuania**.

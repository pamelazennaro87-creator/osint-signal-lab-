# Case 003 — Initial Source Register

## S1 — Europol, Digital Era report

**Type:** Primary institutional source  
**Use:** Online recruitment mechanisms in human trafficking.

Europol documents active recruitment through false job advertisements and fake employment agencies, as well as passive recruitment in which recruiters approach people who publicly seek work abroad.

**Evidence level:** High for the existence of the documented mechanisms; not evidence that any particular advertisement is trafficking.

## S2 — Europol, 2025 criminal ecosystem overview

**Type:** Primary institutional source  
**Use:** Current evolution of online recruitment, digital control, false job opportunities and exploitation.

**Evidence level:** High for Europol's threat assessment; individual-case attribution requires separate evidence.

## Source-independence rule

Repeated reporting of the same Europol assessment is not independent corroboration.

For every future case example, record:
- original source;
- jurisdiction;
- date;
- recruitment mechanism;
- evidence linking recruitment to exploitation;
- whether the source is primary or derivative.

## Planned cross-jurisdiction set

The case will compare official evidence from multiple jurisdictions only after checking:
- legal definitions;
- recording practices;
- victim-count methodology;
- reporting period;
- detection pathway;
- whether online recruitment is explicitly coded.

Cross-country differences must not be treated as direct prevalence differences without methodological qualification.

## 8. Analytical takeaways for Case 005

1. **Two-theatre kill recruitment:** US (Victim-1, $40k) and Lithuania (Victim-2, $25k) share Khrameev command and low-cost photo tasking, different recruiters and recruits.  
2. **Durruthy dual use:** EU arson logistics **and** pre-surveillance media for Victim-1 — strongest person-level bridge between Case 004 field story and Case 005 US plot.  
3. **Refusal pattern:** Both Resident-1 and U.S. Citizen-1 reportedly completed surveillance and declined to murder personally — consistent with “disposable” task layering (recon ≠ execution).  
4. **Spanish-speaking recruiter pool:** Suarez (Cuban) + Castro (Venezuelan) + Resident-1 (Venezuelan in Brooklyn) mirrors Case 004’s Latin American recruitment surface, now aimed at a US murder market rather than EU arson.

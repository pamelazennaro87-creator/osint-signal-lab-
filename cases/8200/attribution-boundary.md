# Attribution Boundary

## Purpose

This document defines the point at which the investigation must stop making identity claims.

The subject is an officer represented in public institutional material by the designation **H׳**.

## Allowed

The investigation may document:
- publicly stated rank
- publicly stated role
- publicly stated unit
- appointment dates
- institutional announcements
- historical roles explicitly attributed by authoritative sources
- publicly documented organizational relationships
- source-to-source propagation
- contradictions
- uncertainty

## Not established by inference alone

The following are not proof:
- same initial
- same rank
- same unit
- compatible career path
- same appointment period
- professional proximity
- appearance in related organizational material
- a third-party claim without independent corroboration

## Identity rule

> **Same designation ≠ same person.**

## Organizational rule

> **Unit-level activity ≠ individual responsibility.**

## Relationship rule

> **Professional proximity ≠ personal relationship.**

## Temporal rule

> **Same appointment cycle ≠ personal relationship.**

## Stop condition

If additional research produces only compatible evidence but no authoritative identity linkage, person-level attribution remains:

**UNRESOLVED**

That is a valid research result.

# Unit 8200 — Public Selection and Training Pathways

Public recruiting material shows multiple pathways. There is no evidence for one universal 8200 selection pipeline.

## GAMA Cyber
The official Mitgaisim page describes a multi-stage process including:

1. online knowledge questionnaire
2. test day
3. practical workshop
4. professional interview
5. further selection stages described by the recruiting process
6. training
7. assignment

The practical workshop is conducted on a closed network not connected to the Internet.

Source: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/roles/מסלול-גאמ-א-סייבר/
Evidence: E2.

## Shchakim
The official page describes a combined set of intelligence tracks, initial screening, tests/interviews and situational assessments, followed by training and assignment.

Source: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/roles/מסלול-שחקים/
Evidence: E2.

## Strong Current — 8200
The official page describes screening, a basic technical course, specialist training and possible later progression into command, instruction and officer training. It states that placement depends on personal data, preferences, professional considerations and Army needs.

Source: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/roles/מסלול-זרם-חזק-8200/
Evidence: E2.

## Pre-service technology education
Official recruiting material describes technology education programs whose graduates can receive invitations to selection processes for elite technological units including 8200.

Source: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/edutech/index.html
Evidence: E2.

## Analytical boundary
These are documented recruitment routes, not a reconstructed personal route for H׳. The public sources do not establish that H׳ entered 8200 through any particular pathway.

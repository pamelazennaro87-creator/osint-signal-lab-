# Case 001 — Unit 8200

## Identity Attribution Under Deliberate Anonymity

### Research question

> **What publicly documented professional trajectory can be established for the officer designated as H׳, without defeating deliberate anonymity?**

## Scope

This case examines public institutional records concerning Unit 8200 and an officer designated **H׳**.

It does not attempt to identify the officer by inference.

The investigation focuses on the boundary between:

**role attribution → identity attribution → biographical attribution**

These are separate analytical problems.

## The critical boundary

Both records use the designation **H׳**.

But neither record explicitly states that the two records concern the same person.

Therefore:

**Identity continuity = UNRESOLVED**

The investigation must not silently convert designation continuity into person-level identification.

## Analytical levels

### Level 1 — Role
Can the role be verified? **Yes.**

### Level 2 — Public designation
Can the designation H׳ be verified? **Yes.**

### Level 3 — Person
Can the designation be connected to a specific named individual? **Not established by the cited official records.**

### Level 4 — Biography
Can education, previous commands, personal relationships, or a complete career be attributed? **Only where independently documented.**

## False Confirmation Test

> **Does the source actually identify the same person?**

If not, the relationship remains unresolved.

The investigation documents the maximum defensible public record while preserving deliberate anonymity.

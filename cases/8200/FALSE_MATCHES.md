# False Matches

A strong OSINT case does not only collect supporting evidence. It actively searches for evidence that could produce a false attribution.

| Potential match | Why it looks compatible | Why it is insufficient |
|---|---|---|
| Another officer designated H׳ | Same public designation | Designation alone does not establish identity |
| Another 8200 officer | Same organizational unit | Unit membership is not person-level identity |
| Another colonel | Same rank | Rank is not unique |
| Another former K. Agam | Same role | Role succession creates multiple possible holders |
| Similar career sequence | Compatible trajectory | Similar careers do not prove the same individual |

## False-confirmation test

For every proposed link:
1. What source makes the link?
2. Does it explicitly identify the person?
3. Is it primary or derivative?
4. Is there an independent source?
5. Does the timeline permit continuity?
6. Is there a competing candidate?
7. What evidence would falsify the link?

A candidate remains **UNRESOLVED** when evidence is compatible but not identifying.

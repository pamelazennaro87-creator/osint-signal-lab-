This timeline records only events supported by cited public sources.

## 2024-09 — Commander resignation
Brig. Gen. Yossi Sariel resigns command of Unit 8200, accepting responsibility for the unit’s part in the 7 October intelligence failure. Public reporting places the resignation in mid-September 2024.

Evidence: E2 / major press.
See also: command-continuity note in cross-intelligence.

## 2024-11-22 — Appointment of Col. (res.) Dr. A as next commander
Defense Minister Israel Katz / IDF announce appointment of Col. (res.) Dr. A as the next commander of Unit 8200. Public reporting states that A had previously served as **deputy commander** of the unit, left service about two–three years earlier, returned from reserve after October 7 to help stabilise the unit, and declined a high-tech role to take the command. He is to be promoted to brigadier general.

Evidence: E2 / institutional press (Jerusalem Post, Israel Hayom, Maariv).
Cross-ref: `cross-intelligence/command-continuity-2024-2025.md`

## 2025-08 — Azure / mass-surveillance reporting
Guardian / +972 / Local Call report that Unit 8200 used Microsoft Azure (custom area) to store and analyse large volumes of intercepted Palestinian phone calls, with primary storage reported in the **Netherlands** and secondary in **Ireland**. Scale cited in the order of thousands of terabytes / hundreds of millions of hours of audio. Rapid data movement out of the Netherlands reported after publication.

Evidence: secondary investigative + later Microsoft confirmation of elements.
Cross-ref: `cross-intelligence/eu-jurisdictional-seam.md`

## 2025-09-25 — Microsoft ceases specified cloud and AI services
Microsoft states it has ceased and disabled specified IMOD / Unit 8200-related cloud storage and AI services after finding evidence supporting elements of the Guardian reporting (including Azure storage capacity in the Netherlands and use of AI services). Reporting indicates a planned or ongoing pivot toward other cloud providers (AWS cited by sources).

Evidence: E2 (Microsoft public statements) + secondary.
Cross-ref: `cross-intelligence/eu-jurisdictional-seam.md`, `microsoft-alumni-friction.md`

## 2026-06 — Microsoft external review closed
Microsoft publishes a final update on the external review; factual findings on the service limitations stand; governance and oversight measures described for future national-security-related engagements.

Evidence: E2 (Microsoft).

## Timeline conclusion
The public record supports an institutional timeline of command, role appointments, cloud dependency, EU jurisdictional exposure, and service termination. It does not support collapsing all partially anonymized records into one person-level network.

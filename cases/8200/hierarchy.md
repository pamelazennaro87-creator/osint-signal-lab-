# Unit 8200 — Publicly Documented Hierarchy Model

This is not a complete internal organizational chart. It is a model of functions and roles explicitly visible in public IDF material.

## Organizational levels

IDF → Intelligence Directorate (Aman) → Unit 8200

Within the publicly documented 8200 structure, the following role categories are visible:

- Commander
- Deputy Commander
- Deputy Commander for Digital
- Head of Unit Staff
- Center Commanders

## Functional model

IDF
  └─ Intelligence Directorate (Aman)
      └─ Unit 8200
          ├─ Commander
          ├─ Deputy Commander
          │   └─ Deputy Commander for Digital
          ├─ Head of Unit Staff
          └─ Centers
              └─ Center Commanders

## What this does not claim

- complete internal reporting lines
- that every center reports directly to a deputy
- personal relationships between officers
- that identical designations represent the same person

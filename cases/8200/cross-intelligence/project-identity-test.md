# Project Identity Test — Intelligence-LLM ↔ The Studio Arabic LLM

**Status:** 🔵 Strongly linked, not explicitly established  
**Case:** 001 — Unit 8200  
**Research question:** Is the Arabic LLM reported as developed by “The Studio” in April 2025 the same project/training program presented by Chaked Roger Joseph Sayedoff in September 2024 as the “first intelligence-LLM”?

## Bottom line

Public evidence strongly links the two descriptions, but no public primary document located in this investigation explicitly states that The Studio was the organizational container for Sayedoff's specific September 2024 training run.

The correct classification is therefore:

> **🔵 STRONGLY LINKED BUT NOT EXPLICITLY ESTABLISHED**

This is stronger than a thematic resemblance, but weaker than a formally documented project identity.

## Direct evidence

### 1. September 2024 — Sayedoff presents the intelligence-LLM

The DefenseML event page lists:

- **25 September 2024**
- **“Training the first intelligence-LLM”**
- **Chaked Roger Joseph Sayedoff**
- session description: training a proprietary foundation model across the organization's text databases, including data selection, model-quality evaluation and perplexity.

Source:
https://machinelearning.co.il/lp-events/defenseml/

### 2. March 2025 — the public talk is tied to the 8200 LLM project

The Guardian/+972/Local Call investigation states that the large-language-model effort was partially revealed through a public talk by Sayedoff, who said he had overseen the project. The reporting describes an 8200 LLM trained on spoken Arabic material and says the model was still being trained in the second half of 2024.

Source:
https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/mar/06/israel-military-ai-surveillance

The Jerusalem Post independently repeats the connection between Sayedoff's 2024 presentation and the 8200 LLM project, including the reported ~100-billion-word training corpus and the fact that deployment had not been established.

Source:
https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-845128

### 3. April 2025 — The Studio is explicitly linked to an Arabic LLM

Reporting on the New York Times investigation describes **The Studio** as an innovation hub inside Unit 8200 that connected serving personnel with reservists from technology companies. Three Israeli officers familiar with the program were cited as saying that one Studio-developed tool was an Arabic large language model capable of handling spoken-Arabic dialects.

The same reporting says the model was developed in the early months of the war and was connected to a chatbot and multimedia databases.

Source:
https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-using-ai-to-pinpoint-hamas-leaders-find-hostages-in-gaza-tunnels-report/

A New York Times republication preserves the same attribution and description:
https://gvwire.com/2025/04/26/israels-ai-experiments-in-the-gaza-war-raise-ethical-concerns/

## Divergent / opposing evidence

### A. The Studio may be broader than this one LLM

The Studio is reported as an innovation hub that matched experts with multiple AI projects. The Arabic LLM is described as **one** tool developed there.

Therefore:

> The Studio → Arabic LLM does not logically imply The Studio = the entire LLM program.

The hub may have been an organizational mechanism around several projects.

**Classification:** 🟡 compatible alternative.

### B. No public primary source explicitly names the Studio in Sayedoff's presentation

The DefenseML event page identifies the talk and its subject but does not identify The Studio.

**Classification:** 🔴 unresolved.

### C. Company involvement must not be inflated

Reporting says reservists worked at companies including Google, Microsoft and Meta. Google stated that the reservists' work was not connected to Google as a company; Meta and Microsoft declined comment in the cited reporting.

Therefore:

> employee expertise / reserve service ≠ company contract or company authorship.

**Classification:** 🔵 documented distinction.

### D. AI21 must remain separate

Public material reports participation by AI21 personnel/reservists, but the evidence located here does not establish an AI21 corporate contract or that AI21 authored the military model.

**Classification:** 🔴 formal company authorship unresolved.

### E. Deployment remains a separate question

The March 2025 investigation reported that the large model was still being trained in the second half of 2024 and that it was unclear whether it had been deployed.

The later Studio reporting describes an Arabic model and chatbot, but does not provide a public model identifier, deployment record, model card, benchmark, or technical architecture that closes the gap.

**Classification:** 🔴 deployment of the specific large model remains unresolved.

## False-confirmation tests

### Test 1 — Same unit
**Result:** insufficient.

8200 appears in all relevant reporting, but unit-level coincidence cannot prove project identity.

### Test 2 — Same language
**Result:** insufficient alone.

Arabic/spoken Arabic is highly discriminating, but not unique enough to prove that two undisclosed models are identical.

### Test 3 — Same period
**Result:** strong supporting signal, not proof.

The 2024 training presentation and 2025 Studio reporting overlap in a plausible development window.

### Test 4 — Same technical problem
**Result:** strong supporting signal.

The spoken-Arabic data problem appears in the presentation/reporting and again in Studio reporting.

### Test 5 — Same use case
**Result:** supporting signal.

The reported Q&A/chatbot concept is compatible with the foundation-model project, but public sources do not expose the exact task specification.

### Test 6 — Explicit source-to-source identity statement
**Result:** not found.

No located public source says, in substance, “the model developed by The Studio is the model presented by Sayedoff at DefenseML.”

## Divergent analytical branch

A different interpretation remains possible:

**Branch A — same project**
Sayedoff's foundation-model training → continued development → Studio provides organizational/talent mechanism → Arabic LLM/chatbot reported in 2025.

**Branch B — related projects**
Sayedoff's foundation model and the Studio Arabic LLM were parallel or overlapping 8200 projects using similar data and personnel.

**Branch C — broader program**
The Studio was a hub supporting multiple AI projects, while the Sayedoff model was one component among them.

Current public evidence does not cleanly eliminate B or C.

The strongest reconstruction is therefore **A/B boundary, with A strongly supported but not formally proven**.

## Evidence rule

> **A missing edge is evidence about the limits of the reconstruction, not permission to invent the edge.**

The investigation therefore records the link without converting it into a certainty.

# Cloud Governance Trace

## A previously underweighted bridge

The foreign connection is not limited to intelligence liaison.

Public reporting in 2025 documented that Unit 8200's cloud relationship with Microsoft began after a November 2021 meeting in Seattle between Microsoft's CEO Satya Nadella and then-8200 commander Yossi Sariel. Reporting based on leaked Microsoft documents described plans to place large volumes of intelligence data in Azure infrastructure in Europe. Microsoft disputed important aspects of the reporting and later said its review found evidence supporting elements concerning Azure storage capacity in the Netherlands and use of AI services.

Sources:
- Guardian investigation, 6 Aug 2025
- +972 Magazine, 2025
- CBS News, 26 Sep 2025

## The unusual part

The sequence creates a trace that crosses four domains:

1. military intelligence leadership;
2. a US technology company's executive level;
3. European cloud infrastructure;
4. later corporate governance intervention.

In September 2025 Microsoft announced that it had ceased and disabled a set of services to a unit within the Israeli Ministry of Defense after its review. Reporting also stated that sources believed data was being moved from the Netherlands and that a possible AWS transfer was being considered; neither the IDF nor Amazon confirmed that transfer in the cited reporting.

## Why this changes the case

This is not evidence that any anonymized 8200 officer participated in the project.

It does, however, demonstrate that the organizational footprint of 8200 can cross national borders without a traditional intelligence liaison channel.

The graph therefore needs an edge type for:

**foreign commercial infrastructure / governance**

distinct from:

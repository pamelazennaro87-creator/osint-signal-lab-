# Cross-Intelligence Attribution Conflicts

## Why this matters

Public reporting can assign the same operation, capability, or outcome to different intelligence organizations.

The laboratory records competing attributions instead of selecting one without sufficient evidence.

## Example pattern

Former senior 8200 personnel have publicly discussed the relationship between 8200, Mossad and Shin Bet and have disputed or qualified some public operational attributions.

Such statements are valuable first-person evidence but remain attributed claims unless independently corroborated.

## Method

For every disputed attribution:

1. identify the earliest located claim;
2. identify the organization named by each source;
3. map source genealogy;
4. check for official confirmation or denial;
5. distinguish capability evidence from actor-identification evidence;
6. preserve unresolved status where the evidence does not close the attribution.

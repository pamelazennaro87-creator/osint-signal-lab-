# 8200 Global Bridge Map

## Scope

This map records public evidence of institutional and post-service links extending beyond Israel. It does not assign foreign relationships to any anonymized officer.

## Bridge 1 — United States intelligence

The Washington Post reported that serving and former senior 8200 personnel described direct working-level relationships and intelligence sharing with US intelligence. The report also describes regular interaction between 8200 personnel and Mossad and Shin Bet personnel.

Source: https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2024/06/14/us-israel-intelligence-cia-hostages/

Classification: institutional / working-level evidence.

## Bridge 2 — UK intelligence

Academic research on Anglo-Israeli intelligence relations documents information sharing between GCHQ, NSA and Unit 8200 on shared Middle East interests, including Iran-related intelligence.

Source: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09592296.2021.1961489

Classification: academic / institutional.

## Bridge 3 — Global technology sector

Reuters describes the post-service trajectory of 8200 personnel into Israel's technology and cybersecurity sector. A Reuters report on Wiz also documents that founder Assaf Rappaport served in 8200 and that several co-founders had served together in the unit.

Source: https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/what-is-israels-secretive-cyber-warfare-unit-8200-2024-09-18/

Classification: public alumni / technology bridge.

## Bridge 4 — United States technology ecosystem

Public reporting documents 8200 alumni among founders and leaders of internationally active cybersecurity companies, including Palo Alto Networks and Wiz. This creates a separate post-service network connecting the military-intelligence talent pipeline to the US-listed and US-facing technology market.

This is a structural alumni bridge, not evidence that active 8200 officers participate in those companies.

## Analytical model

The public evidence supports a layered graph:

Israel: AMAN -> 8200 -> Mossad / Shin Bet

Foreign intelligence: 8200 <-> US intelligence; 8200 <-> UK intelligence

Post-service economy: 8200 alumni -> Israeli/global technology companies -> US-facing market

These are different edge types and must not be collapsed into a single personal network.

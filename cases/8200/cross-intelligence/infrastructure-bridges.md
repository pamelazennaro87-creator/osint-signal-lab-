# 8200 Infrastructure Bridges

## A different kind of foreign connection

Not every international link is a liaison officer, intelligence agency or diplomatic posting.

Public reporting published in August 2025 described a Microsoft Azure cloud project associated with Unit 8200 and stated that relevant infrastructure was hosted in Microsoft data centres in the Netherlands and Ireland. The reporting was based on leaked documents and interviews; Microsoft disputed aspects of the characterization and said Azure was used for defensive cyber operations.

Source: The Guardian / +972 Magazine / Local Call, 6 August 2025.

## Analytical significance

This creates a distinct edge type:

**8200 -> commercial cloud provider -> European data-centre infrastructure**

It should not be merged with:

**8200 -> foreign intelligence liaison**

The two are analytically different.

## Why this matters

A modern intelligence organization can create international exposure through:

- cloud infrastructure;
- software suppliers;
- data-processing locations;
- technical contractors;
- commercial technology ecosystems;
- former personnel.

Those links can exist even when no foreign intelligence officer is publicly named.

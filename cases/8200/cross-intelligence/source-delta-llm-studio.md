# Source Delta — Intelligence-LLM ↔ The Studio

## Purpose

This document records what each source independently contributes to the reconstruction and prevents the same journalistic genealogy from being counted as multiple independent confirmations.

## Primary

### DefenseML — 25 September 2024

The official event page lists Sayedoff's session:

**“Training the first intelligence-LLM”**

The description says the talk concerned training a proprietary foundation model across the organization's text databases, with discussion of data selection, model evaluation and perplexity.

Source:
https://machinelearning.co.il/lp-events/defenseml/

**Value:** direct primary record of the public presentation and its stated subject.

**Limit:** the event page does not identify The Studio or provide a model identifier.

## Secondary — March 2025

### Guardian / +972 / Local Call

Joint investigation reporting links Sayedoff's public presentation to the 8200 LLM project and reports:

- spoken-Arabic focus;
- large-scale Arabic training data;
- approximately 100 billion words reported;
- development accelerated after October 2023;
- model still being trained in the second half of 2024;
- deployment not established publicly.

Source:
https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/mar/06/israel-military-ai-surveillance

**Genealogy note:** Guardian, +972 and Local Call are one joint investigation, not three independent confirmations.

### Jerusalem Post

Follow-up reporting repeats the Sayedoff/project connection and the uncertainty around deployment.

Source:
https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-845128

**Value:** independent publication reproducing/adding context; not independent primary evidence.

## Secondary — April 2025

### New York Times / republication

Reporting identifies The Studio as an 8200 innovation hub connecting serving personnel and technology-company reservists. It reports that one Studio-developed tool was an Arabic large language model and describes a chatbot/multimedia-search use case.

Source:
https://gvwire.com/2025/04/26/israels-ai-experiments-in-the-gaza-war-raise-ethical-concerns/

Additional coverage:
https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-using-ai-to-pinpoint-hamas-leaders-find-hostages-in-gaza-tunnels-report/

**Value:** introduces the organizational node “The Studio” and explicitly links it to an Arabic LLM.

**Limit:** no public technical document located here identifies the exact model, weights, benchmark, training run, or direct connection to Sayedoff's DefenseML session.

## Methodological note

The apparent convergence is meaningful because the sources share multiple identifiers: intelligence-LLM, Arabic/spoken Arabic, 8200, 2023–2024 development window, large-scale training, and later chatbot/Q&A reporting.

But convergence is not identity.

The lab therefore records the connection as **🔵 strongly linked, not explicitly established**.

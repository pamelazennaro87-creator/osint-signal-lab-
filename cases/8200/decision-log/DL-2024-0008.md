# Decision Log DL-2024-0008

## Decision

Classify the relationship between Sayedoff's September 2024 “Training the first intelligence-LLM” presentation and the Arabic LLM later attributed to The Studio as:

> **🔵 STRONGLY LINKED BUT NOT EXPLICITLY ESTABLISHED**

## Why

The evidence converges on:

- the same broad intelligence-LLM project;
- Unit 8200;
- Arabic/spoken-Arabic specialization;
- a development window spanning 2023–2024;
- large-scale training;
- a later chatbot/Q&A use case;
- Sayedoff's reported oversight of the LLM project;
- The Studio's reported role in developing an Arabic LLM.

## Why not green

No public primary document located in this investigation explicitly states that:

1. The Studio was the organizational container for Sayedoff's exact DefenseML training run; or
2. the later Studio Arabic LLM has the same model identity, checkpoint, architecture, or weights as the model described in September 2024.

## Divergent branches retained

### Branch A — Same project
Most compatible reconstruction.

### Branch B — Related projects
Two overlapping 8200 Arabic-LLM efforts sharing data, personnel, or infrastructure.

### Branch C — Broader Studio program
The Studio was a multi-project innovation hub and the Sayedoff model was only one component.

B and C cannot currently be eliminated from public evidence.

## Important separation

The following claims remain distinct:

**Training ≠ deployment**

**Employee reserve service ≠ company authorship**

**AI21 personnel participation ≠ AI21 corporate contract**

**The Studio developing an Arabic LLM ≠ The Studio being proven as the owner/container of Sayedoff's exact training run**

**Q&A intent ≠ demonstrated operational production system**

## Reassessment trigger

Upgrade to 🟢 only if a public primary or directly attributable source explicitly connects the Studio Arabic LLM to Sayedoff's September 2024 training project.

Upgrade deployment status only if a source documents operational use of the specific large model, not merely other 8200 AI systems.

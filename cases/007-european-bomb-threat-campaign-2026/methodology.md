# Methodology, privacy and safety

## Scope

Lawful, publicly accessible sources only. This case documents reported incidents and assesses the public evidence. It does not attempt to identify private individuals or access systems.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Police, prosecutors, courts, emergency services, affected institutions and university statements.
2. News agencies with direct reporting and named attribution.
3. Established news organisations citing primary sources.
4. Specialist technical analysis, labelled as such.
5. Social media and anonymous claims as leads only, never stand-alone proof.

## Keep identity layers separate

- Display name in an email client.
- Visible From address.
- Reply-To and Return-Path.
- Authenticated sending domain.
- Registered account holder.
- Person or process that accessed the account.
- Human operator who composed or initiated the message.

A mailbox associated with a person or institution does not establish that person's involvement.

## Correlation standard

A repeated alias, wording, threat theme, timing or target type is a lead, not proof of common authorship. Stronger linkage requires independently verified technical indicators, original message data, provider records obtained lawfully, or official findings.

Do not infer state sponsorship, nationality, ideology or organisational membership from a name, language, time zone, domain suffix or symbolic reference.

## Email forensics boundary

Original .eml files and full headers may help assess routing and authentication, but headers can be incomplete or altered by forwarding and intermediaries. SPF, DKIM, DMARC, Received, Message-ID and Authentication-Results must be interpreted in context.

This project will not attempt account access, bypass authentication, scan private systems, obtain provider logs or deanonymise people. Provider logs and subscriber data are for competent authorities acting under lawful process.

## Publication safeguards

- Do not publish names of private account holders or personal contact details.
- Do not upload original threat emails, attachments, credentials or private headers to this public repository.
- Redact personal data from screenshots and documents.
- Do not reproduce actionable threat details beyond what is necessary to document the public event.
- Preserve uncertainty and record corrections in the changelog.

## Confidence vocabulary

CONFIRMED (by a primary record) · REPORTED · CORROBORATED · INFERRED · UNRESOLVED · CONTRADICTED.

# Subcase — Media and aviation-related reports

ANSA reported that RTE in Ireland and Nova TV in Bulgaria received threatening emails signed “Mikhail Andropov” on 6 November 2026, reportedly with subject “Message to the West”.

Source:
- https://www.ansa.it/europa/notizie/rubriche/altrenews/2026/11/06/media-il-mittente-delle-email-sugli-allarmi-bomba-si-firma-mikhail-andropov_f1063afb-8175-40e9-ac4b-70f41dd3635e.html

Reuters reported that it could not establish whether aviation-related threats and the later media messages came from the same source:
- https://www.reuters.com/world/least-300-italian-schools-evacuated-after-email-bomb-alerts-2026-11-11/

## Assessment

The alias is a reported point of correlation. A sender's own claim of responsibility is not independent corroboration. Keep aviation incidents separate until primary evidence or official findings establish a link.

# Subcase — Italian school alerts, 11 November 2026

Reuters and ANSA reported mass threat emails to schools in Italy on 11 November 2026, resulting in evacuations and security checks. The message was reported as signed “Mikhail Andropov” and included the phrase “Vilayat of Misoginistan”.

## Assessment

The incident and reported signature are documented in public reporting. The signature does not establish a civil identity or real organisation. Public reporting does not provide the original message headers for independent technical examination.

# Subcase — Dayeh University account lead

Taiwan's Central News Agency reported that Dayeh University detected unusually large-scale sending from an account associated with a former student. The university suspended the account and began an investigation; the cause and true origin of the sending were not established in the report.

Source:
- https://money.udn.com/money/story/5599/9872763?from=udnamp-referralnews_ch1001artbottom

## Limits

- This is an infrastructure lead, not proof against the account holder.
- Public reports contain inconsistent renderings of the account-associated name; this case omits the private individual's name.
- The Taiwan lead does not prove that messages received in other countries used the same account or operator.

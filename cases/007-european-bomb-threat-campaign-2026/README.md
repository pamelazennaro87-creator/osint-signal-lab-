# Case 007 — European Bomb Threat Campaign (2026)

**Status:** Preliminary public-source investigation  
**Research cut-off:** 11 November 2026  
**Researcher:** Independent OSINT research  
**Classification:** Public information only; no attribution established

## Research question

What can be established from public sources about the cross-border bomb-threat emails reported in Europe in November 2026, and what evidence would be required to determine whether the incidents share an operator or infrastructure?

## Executive assessment

Public reporting documents mass threat emails affecting schools in Italy and similar alerts in parts of the UK and Ireland. The signature **“Mikhail Andropov”** was reported in messages to media outlets on 6 November and schools on 11 November. Taiwan's Central News Agency reported that Dayeh University detected anomalous mass sending from an account associated with a former student, suspended it and began an investigation.

These facts do **not** establish the sender's civil identity, the human operator of the account, or a common operator behind all incidents. The Dayeh account is an investigative lead, not evidence against its holder. No public evidence cited in this case establishes state sponsorship or membership in a real organisation called “Vilayat of Misoginistan”.

## Analytical boundary

Keep distinct: display name, visible sender address, authenticated sending account, account owner, person who accessed the account, and human operator. Similar wording, an alias, or a geographic domain suffix is not sufficient to attribute authorship.

## Current conclusion

**UNRESOLVED.** A cross-border pattern is documented in public reporting; a single actor, common technical origin, and identity remain unproven. The conclusion must not be stronger than the evidence.

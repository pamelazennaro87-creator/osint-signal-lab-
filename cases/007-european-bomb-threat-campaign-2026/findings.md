# Findings — public reporting

This file records narrow claims supported by the listed public sources. “Reported” does not mean independently forensically verified by this project.

## F-01 — Italian school alerts

Reuters and ANSA reported mass threat emails to Italian schools on 11 November 2026, evacuations or security checks, and the signature “Mikhail Andropov”.

Sources:
- https://www.reuters.com/world/least-300-italian-schools-evacuated-after-email-bomb-alerts-2026-11-11/
- https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/cronaca/2026/11/11/allarme-bomba-vi-annienteremo-la-mail-inviata-a-oltre-cento-scuole_f222b6df-f984-46ac-9192-6746f70c4074.html

## F-02 — Similar school alerts outside Italy

Reuters reported similar messages in Scotland, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland. PSNI separately reported widespread school receipt of a threat email and said it originated outside the UK.

Sources:
- https://www.reuters.com/world/least-300-italian-schools-evacuated-after-email-bomb-alerts-2026-11-11/
- https://www.psni.police.uk/

## F-03 — Andropov signature in media messages

ANSA reported that RTE in Ireland and Nova TV in Bulgaria received emails signed “Mikhail Andropov” on 6 November, with subject “Message to the West”.

Source:
- https://www.ansa.it/europa/notizie/rubriche/altrenews/2026/11/06/media-il-mittente-delle-email-sugli-allarmi-bomba-si-firma-mikhail-andropov_f1063afb-8175-40e9-ac4b-70f41dd3635e.html

## F-04 — Dayeh University account investigation

Taiwan's Central News Agency reported that Dayeh University detected unusually large-scale sending from an account associated with a former student, suspended it and started an investigation. The cause and true origin were not established in that report.

Source:
- https://money.udn.com/money/story/5599/9872763?from=udnamp-referralnews_ch1001artbottom

## F-05 — No public attribution

Reuters and AP reported no established public attribution of the school threat campaign to a specific group or government.

Sources:
- https://www.reuters.com/world/least-300-italian-schools-evacuated-after-email-bomb-alerts-2026-11-11/
- https://apnews.com/article/0f312fda4ee2e9cdcebd0d6e1a40d9e8

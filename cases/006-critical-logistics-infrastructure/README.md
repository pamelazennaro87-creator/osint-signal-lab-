# Case 006 — Critical Logistics & Dual-Use Infrastructure

## Research question

How do publicly documented hybrid operations select and probe **logistics networks** and **dual-use manufacturing** sites in Europe, and what evidence boundaries separate a courier incident, a factory yard arson attempt, and a strategic infrastructure target?

## Why this case exists

Cases 004 and 005 map **who is recruited** and **who is charged**.  
Case 006 maps the **target surface**:

- international courier / air-cargo nodes;
- dual-use or Ukraine-support manufacturing;
- energy and transport infrastructure named in open tasking talk;
- the difference between a *test package*, a *failed arson*, and a *systemic logistics attack*.

This case does **not** re-litigate person graphs. It borrows named incidents only as **target events** and keeps person-level identity in 004/005.

## Core analytical distinctions

1. **Carrier vs payload** — attacking DHL’s network ≠ attacking a named shipper’s goods.  
2. **Dual-use manufacturer vs generic warehouse** — TVC-type targets carry political signalling; parcel bombs test routing and detection.  
3. **Test package vs operational device** — Eurojust “test packages” to US/Canada are a research object of their own.  
4. **Failed ignition vs successful disruption** — court outcomes often turn on completion and knowledge, not only intent labels.

## Alternative hypotheses

- **H1 — Logistics as force multiplier:** low-cost recruits + courier networks = scalable disruption without SOF teams.  
- **H2 — Signalling targets:** Ukraine-linked dual-use sites chosen for narrative effect more than tonnage destroyed.  
- **H3 — Opportunistic crime labelled hybrid:** some fires are criminal or insurance-driven and later absorbed into intelligence narratives (false-merge risk).  
- **H4 — Corporate security gap:** multinationals (integrators, shippers) are the soft underbelly, not the state.

## False-confirmation tests

- Same courier brand in two incidents ≠ same cell.  
- “Ukraine-linked company” ≠ proof of GRU tasking without primary edge.  
- Substation mentioned in chat ≠ attack executed.  
- Leipzig fire ≠ proof of intent to down an aircraft unless primary sources establish loading timeline and device design.

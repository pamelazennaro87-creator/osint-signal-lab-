# Reproducibility

## Minimum investigation record

Every completed case should preserve:

1. research question;
2. scope and date;
3. claim register;
4. source URLs;
5. source type;
6. source genealogy;
7. relevant dates;
8. extracted evidence;
9. contradiction register;
10. alternative hypotheses;
11. confidence;
12. limitations;
13. reassessment triggers.

## Volatile sources

For changing pages, record:

- access date;
- page title;
- relevant text;
- official identifier where available;
- archived copy or permitted evidence snapshot where lawful.

## Dynamic data

AIS, sanctions lists, corporate records and other changing datasets should be treated as **time-indexed evidence**.

A current record must not silently overwrite a historical state.

## AI assistance

AI may assist with:

- discovery;
- translation;
- classification;
- comparison;
- extraction;
- graph structuring.

AI-generated assertions must not become evidence without human/source verification.

## Reproduction test

Another researcher should be able to understand:

**what was searched → why a source was selected → what claim it supports → what remains unknown.**

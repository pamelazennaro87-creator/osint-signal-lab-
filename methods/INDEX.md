## Design Intent

The methods exist to force three disciplines that most public OSINT work collapses:

1. **Source Genealogy** — origin and reproduction path of claims
2. **Temporal Continuity** — validity of roles, relationships and statuses across time
3. **False-Match Discipline** — active testing of apparently compatible records

If a case does not stress at least one of these dimensions, it is incomplete.

---

## Principle

> The conclusion must never be stronger than the evidence that supports it.
